# My Favorite Movie: Harry Potter

## Overview

**Harry Potter** is a *fantasy film series* based on the novels by J.K. Rowling. It follows a young wizard who discovers he is famous in the magical world and attends **Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry**. The series ran for 8 films, from 2001 to 2011.

---

## Main Characters

1. Harry Potter
2. Hermione Granger
3. Ron Weasley
4. Albus Dumbledore
5. Lord Voldemort

## Why I Love It

- Magical world and creative details
  - Spells, potions, and creatures
  - Hogwarts castle and the moving staircases
- Themes of friendship, courage, and loyalty
- Great soundtrack and cast
